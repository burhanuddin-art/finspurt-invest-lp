Frequently asked questions

Is this a trading service?

No. This is a long-term investment advisory service focused on building wealth over 3 to 5 years, rather than short-term speculative trading.

Do I need to open a new trading or demat account?

No. You can continue investing through your existing broker and demat account. Finspurt provides research-backed recommendations, while all investment decisions and transactions remain under your control.

How often are recommendations shared?

We provide recommendations whenever our screening identifies a research-backed opportunity. This ensures quality over quantity.

Will I receive buy and sell guidance?

Yes. We provide clear entry, hold and exit strategies for every recommendation to help you manage your portfolio.

How much capital do I need to start?

While there is no strict minimum, we recommend starting with at least ₹2,00,000 to properly diversify across the Fortune 20 portfolio.
